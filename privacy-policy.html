Screen Mirroring privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026 · App: Screen Mirroring (app.mirror.receiver) for Android

In short: mirroring happens entirely on your local Wi-Fi network. The app has no accounts and does not send your screen, audio or files to us or anyone else. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to serve them, as described below.

Who we are

Screen Mirroring is published on Google Play by the developer account "Vignesh V". You can contact us at learnnewhere@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Mirrored screen, video and audio travel directly from your Apple device to this app over your local network. They are decoded and shown on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Sharing between Android devices: if you choose to share this phone's screen to another device running Screen Mirroring, Android asks for your permission first and shows that your screen is being captured. The captured screen is encoded on your phone and sent directly to the device you picked on your local network. It is not uploaded or recorded. Sharing stops when you tap Stop or the connection ends.
  • Device discovery: the app announces itself on your local network (mDNS) so your iPhone, iPad, Mac or other Android devices can find it, and looks for other receivers when you share your screen. This stays within your network.
  • Settings (such as the receiver name, PIN option and display options) are stored only on your device.
  • Logs are kept on your device to help with troubleshooting. They leave your device only if you choose to export or share them yourself.
  • Downloaded videos, if you use the download feature, are saved to your device's storage.

We, the developer, do not run any servers for this app and do not receive any of this information.

This data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection and handled under Google's policies. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices and consent

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: before personalised ads are shown, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Privacy settings.
  • US states with privacy laws: where required, the same tool lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads or Google → Ads).

Legal basis (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, personalised advertising is based on your consent. Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and security rely on legitimate interests. Local mirroring does not involve processing of personal data by us.

Permissions the app uses

  • Network and Wi-Fi access, multicast: to receive mirroring and be discoverable on your local network.
  • Nearby devices / local network: required by Android to talk to devices on your network.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to keep the receiver running and show its status.
  • Screen capture (media projection): only when you choose to share this phone's screen with another device. Android asks for your permission each time.
  • Display over other apps and start at boot (optional): to open automatically when a device connects, if you enable it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are limited to content rated suitable for teens and older.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google's policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device ad settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google, but you can always contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
